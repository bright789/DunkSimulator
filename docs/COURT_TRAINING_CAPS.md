# Court Vertical Training Caps v0.1

`CourtConfig` is the sole source of the two training limits and the High School gate:

| Court | Training stops at | Unlock requirement | Cash cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Neighborhood | 75 Vertical | Starting court | $0 |
| High School Gym | 150 Vertical | 75 Vertical | $4,000 once |

These are **training limits, not player stat caps**. Returning to Neighborhood at Vertical 120 never reduces Vertical, dunk unlocks, jump height, or fractional remainder. It only prevents new Vertical Training there. Dunk rewards, court bonuses, TrainingLevel multipliers/prices, challenge rewards, and dunk thresholds are unchanged. No new profile field or schema migration is needed.

`PlayerService.AwardTraining` resolves the player's private CurrentCourt and unlocked courts, falling back to Neighborhood for malformed state. It rejects an already-capped tick before changing progression. A crossing tick uses the existing fractional-unit math but clamps whole Vertical to the court cap. Excess whole points from that tick are discarded; the sub-point remainder stays with the player for training at a higher court. An already-over-cap player keeps the existing remainder unchanged. `TrainingService` stops the hold session and sends a cooldown-limited `TrainingCapNotice` only to that player. The HUD shows `COURT TRAINING MAXED`, the court's limit, and the next step; client UI cannot authorize training.

High School's minimum Vertical changes from 80 to 75 **only** to make the Neighborhood cap and progression gate compatible. Its $6,000 cost remains unchanged and is deducted once. Existing High School unlocks persist, including accounts whose current Vertical no longer matches a later gate. The live portal/menu read the new `CourtConfig` automatically. The **Studio-owned physical portal sign** is generated by `HighSchoolBuilder`, so after syncing and stopping Play, import the latest `tools/HighSchoolBuilder.rbxmx` into ServerStorage (replace only an older imported tool folder), then run `require(game:GetService("ServerStorage").HighSchoolBuilder.Build).Run()` in the edit-mode Command Bar. It replaces only builder-marked scenery and preserves gameplay objects. Save the place. No Neighborhood rebuild is needed. The builder still makes ceiling and overhead doorway geometry non-collidable; Vertical's jump curve is unchanged.

## Manual checks

1. In Neighborhood at Vertical 74 with nonzero training remainder, hold E until the next tick: Vertical ends at **75**, not 76; training stops, the cap toast appears, and no Cash changes.
2. At 75 in Neighborhood, press E again: no gain. Save/rejoin: Vertical remains 75. Save up $6,000 through dunks/challenges; the High School portal must accept 75 Vertical and deduct exactly $6,000 once.
3. Travel to High School; training resumes using the same TrainingLevel and 1.15x court bonus. At 149 plus a fractional carry, crossing ends at **150**, not 151. Subsequent High School ticks give no gain.
4. Return to Neighborhood at 120 or 150: stat, jump, unlocked styles, and existing remainder remain; training gives nothing. Return to High School at 120: training works until 150.
5. Try a client-edited CurrentCourt attribute or remote spoof; neither can grant a better cap or bypass unlocks. Reset/rejoin at either court and confirm persistence. Check a high-Vertical dunk at both courts.

Future College/Pro caps belong in new `CourtConfig` definitions; no speculative future court is shipped here.

## Jump limit per court (2026-09-27)

Each court also caps **how high you jump** at its training cap: Neighborhood 75, High School 150, College 250 and Skyline Rooftop 350. That way a high-Vertical player who travels back to an easier court never jumps through its ceiling.

- **What's kept:** the saved Vertical is never reduced, and dunk unlocks, payouts, leaderboards and Rebirth all keep using the real Vertical.
- **How it works:**
  - `PlayerService.setJump` sets `Humanoid.JumpHeight` from `min(Vertical, court cap)` on join, respawn, training, court travel and Rebirth.
  - It mirrors that value in the `JumpVertical` attribute.
- **HUD:** when the jump is limited, the Vertical card says "VERTICAL - JUMP 150 HERE".
- **Tested with a 322-Vertical profile:**
  - Rooftop jump height: 406 studs.
  - After traveling to the High School: jump limit 150, jump height 111 studs, and a real jump peaked 111 studs above the floor (the ceiling is 150).
  - A Between the Legs dunk there still paid $1,911, the full-Vertical amount.
